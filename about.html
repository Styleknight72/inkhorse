ABOUT INKHORSE

A real book app that goes where you go

Say it. Hear it. Fix it. Publish it. The book app you write with your voice and edit with your ears.

WHAT IT'S FORWriting a book, wherever you are

Inkhorse™ is a free app for writing a book, a story, or a journal, on your phone, tablet or computer. You write page by page, flip through it like a printed book, and listen to it read aloud. When it's ready, you can turn it into an ebook or a print-ready file.

It isn't a notes app and it isn't a toy. It's a writing tool built around one job: helping you finish what you start.

WHO IT'S FORStudents, and anyone with a story

High school students, college students, and anyone in between. If you've ever drafted something in your Notes app because your phone was all you had with you, Inkhorse was made for you.

NovelsShort storiesJournalsClass projectsMemoirsFamily historyPoetry

You don't need to be technical. If you can use a web browser, you can use Inkhorse.

HOW IT WORKSOpen it and start writing

  1. Open getinkhorse.comOn any phone, tablet or computer. No download, no app store, no account to make.
  2. Put it on your Home ScreenIt gets its own horse icon, opens full screen like a real app, and keeps working with no signal.
  3. Write by voice or keyboardDictate or type. The pages build themselves as you go, with page numbers and a running word count.
  4. Listen, fix, repeatHave your chapter read back to you, catch what doesn't sound right, and fix it by ear.
  5. Back it up with one tapSave a backup file to iCloud, Google Drive or wherever you keep files, and bring your book back on a new phone or another device.

WHAT'S INSIDEMore than it looks

  • Hoofprint™ dictation fixesLearns the names in your book, like your family, your town and your characters, and fixes them when phone dictation gets them wrong.
  • Marks for laterHighlight a sentence that needs work and jump from mark to mark when you revise.
  • Drafts shelfWrite loose pages that don't belong anywhere yet, then drop them into the right chapter when they're ready.
  • Measure your bookSee how many words fit on a page for each phone, tablet and paperback size, and roughly how long your whole book runs.
  • Bring in your writingImport a Word, EPUB, PDF or text file and keep writing on it.
  • Read other books tooAdd EPUB, PDF, Word or text books to your shelf and read them with the same page turns and read-aloud.
  • Export when it's readyEPUB for Apple Books and Kindle, a print-ready PDF interior for KDP or IngramSpark, or a Word file for an editor.
  • A built-in manualA picture guide to every button, right inside the app.
