What Hoofprint does under the hood

  1. Builds a word list from your bookIt reads your chapters and collects the names in them: people, places, brands. You can add your own words too.
  2. Checks what dictation typesIt compares groups of one to three words against that list and measures how close the spelling is. That's how it knows bold duck is almost Bolduc.
  3. Learns from your correctionsCorrect a misheard name once, or tap Fix, and Hoofprint remembers. Next time it fixes it for you, with Undo.
  4. Runs on your phoneNo server, no account. Hoofprint does all its work right on your device.
Works on any phoneiPhone or Android, big screen or small. Use Inkhorse's Dictate button or the microphone on your keyboard. Hoofprint checks the words either way.

Put it on your Home Screen

On iPhone or iPad

  1. In Safari, tap Open Inkhorse, so you are on the app page, not this one.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

On Android

  1. In Chrome, tap Open Inkhorse, so you are on the app page.
  2. Tap the menu button (three dots).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app. The wording varies by phone.

Good to know

Your book stays on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy. Save a backup file now and then to iCloud, Google Drive or wherever you keep files, and you can bring your whole book back, even on a new phone.
