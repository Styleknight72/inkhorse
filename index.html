What it does

Write by voice or keyboardDictate a chapter or type it. Your words stay yours.
Listen to your draftRead-aloud with rewind, replay and skip, like a tape deck.
Mark it, fix it laterHighlight a sentence, then jump from mark to mark when you revise.
See the real bookPage-flip preview with book page numbers and a running word count.
Measure your bookSee how many words fit on a page for each phone, tablet and paperback size.
Bring in your own writingImport a Word, EPUB, PDF or text file and read it as real book pages, or keep writing on it.
Export when it's readyEPUB for Apple Books and Kindle, a print-ready PDF interior for KDP or IngramSpark, or a Word manuscript for an editor.

Put it on your Home Screen

  1. In Safari, tap Open Inkhorse above, so you are on the app page, not this one.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Good to know

Your book stays on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy. Save a backup file to Files or iCloud now and then, and you can bring your whole book back, even on a new phone.

Testing Inkhorse?

Found a bug? Tell the person who sent you this link what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot and your phone model help.
