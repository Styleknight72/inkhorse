What Hoofprint does under the hood

  1. Builds a word list from your bookIt reads your chapters and collects the names in them: people, places, brands. You can add your own words too.
  2. Checks what dictation typesIt compares groups of one to three words against that list and measures how close the spelling is. That's how it knows bold duck is almost Bolduc.
  3. Learns from your correctionsCorrect a misheard name once, or tap Fix, and Hoofprint remembers. Next time it fixes it for you, with Undo.
  4. Runs on your phoneNo server, no account. Hoofprint does all its work right on your device.

Install Inkhorse

Put Inkhorse on your Home Screen and it works like an app: its own horse icon, full screen, and it opens even with no signal. It takes about a minute.

Inkhorse is installed.Open it from the horse icon on your Home Screen.
Open Inkhorse, then add it
Follow the pictures below. Your screen may look a little different.
  1. Open Inkhorse in SafariTap Open Inkhorse above, so you're on the app itself, not this page.
  2. Tap ••• then ShareThe three dots are next to the address bar. If you already see the Share button (a square with an up arrow), tap that instead.
  3. Tap Add to Home ScreenScroll down the list to find it. If it isn't there, tap View More.
  4. Keep Open as Web App on, tap AddThat switch makes Inkhorse open full screen like a real app. Leave it on.
  5. Open it from your Home ScreenTap the horse icon. Inkhorse opens full screen, with no Safari bars.
  1. Open Inkhorse in ChromeTap Install Inkhorse above if you see it, and you're done in one tap. Otherwise open Inkhorse, then tap the menu (three dots).
  2. Tap Install appOn some phones it says Add to Home screen. The wording varies by phone.
  3. Tap InstallThe horse icon lands on your Home Screen. Tap it and Inkhorse opens full screen.
No signal? It still opens. Open Inkhorse once with a signal and your phone keeps a copy. After that you can write, read and turn pages anywhere. When you're back online it picks up the newest version on its own.

Good to know

Inkhorse runs on any device, but each book lives on the device you write it on. There is no account and no cloud copy. Save a backup file now and then to iCloud, Google Drive or wherever you keep files. You can open it on another device to keep writing there, or bring your whole book back on a new phone.
